Our story

A family trade, not just a firm.

Building has been our family's work for three generations. The craft was learned on site — watching how a wall is set true, how wood is chosen, how a ceiling should catch the evening light — and handed down from one generation to the next.

Mahesh Services carries that forward today. We are still the people who come to your site, listen to how your family actually lives, and then build it properly: the civil work behind the walls and the interiors you see every day.

Most of our work comes the old-fashioned way — from families we have already built for, telling the next one.

Featured project

Walk through a home we completed.

A complete residence, done turnkey — from the carved entrance door to the balcony garden. Every room was planned around the family who lives in it: where the children read, where the elders sit for pooja, where everyone gathers to eat.

Project walkthrough · 2 min 18 sInterior & civil execution by Mahesh Services
THE WELCOME

An entrance that says “this is home.”

A full-height wooden door with brass elephant handles and bells, a matching shoe console and a hand-lettered Marathi nameplate — so the welcome starts in the lift lobby.

THE LIVING ROOM

Space for the whole family, and the guests.

Recliners for movie nights, a sculpted relief wall behind the TV, backlit niches for the brass and the keepsakes, and a cove ceiling that glows rather than glares.

THE HEART OF IT

A jhula, because some things should stay.

A traditional wooden swing hangs beside a carved stone-finish wall — a modern home that still feels like the homes we grew up in.

DINING & KITCHEN

Where every meal is planned around.

A lotus-motif wall frames the dining table. The kitchen is modular and easy to keep clean, with a place for the copper and brass vessels that are part of the family's story.

ROOMS FOR QUIET

A reading nook and a place to work.

Floor-to-ceiling book shelves with a low cushioned seat for long afternoons, and a live-edge wooden table for work and music practice.

THE BALCONY

A garden and a pooja corner, in the city.

The balcony became the greenest room in the house — planters, cane seating, a rangoli spot and a carved pooja space looking out over the trees.

BEDROOMS & BATH

Each room made for the person in it.

A calm master bedroom with a backlit headboard wall, and a child's room with a hand-finished heart mural and space for every doll and teddy.
